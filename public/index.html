🌼 Why Open Innovation Matters Here

An ofrenda holds the most sacred, intimate artifacts of our lives: photographs of lost ancestors, heartfelt eulogies, and vulnerable family memories.

  • Sacred Privacy: No photos or grief notes are sent to corporate cloud servers for training or surveillance.
  • Eternal Preservation: Cloud APIs shut down or hike prices; open-weight models (Gemma 2 / Llama 3) run on your machine forever with the internet off.
  • Authentic Grief: Closed models enforce sanitized corporate disclaimers; open models allow culturally rich, respectful expressions of love and loss.
